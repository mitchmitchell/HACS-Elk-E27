> [!WARNING]
> **Take a full Home Assistant backup before upgrading** (include the database). Restoring it
> is the only way to roll back to 0.1.x; 0.1.x refuses to load an upgraded entry.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Upgrade in place, one re-link.** From any 0.1.x: back up, update in HACS, restart Home
> Assistant, and enter your access code and passphrase once on the **Relink panel** card.
> Do not delete the integration. Entity IDs, registry IDs, icons, areas, labels, disabled and
> hidden state, automations, dashboards and history are kept. Follow
> [Upgrading to 0.2.1](../../README.md#to-021).

## What's new
- **In-place upgrade from 0.1.x** (config entry VERSION 1 to 2). After the re-link and before
  any entity loads, unique IDs and the panel device identifier are rewritten to the 0.2.x
  identity. It is all or nothing; a conflict changes nothing and raises an
  "Elk E27 upgrade blocked" Repairs issue naming the conflicting entities. Delete them and
  reload the integration to finish.
- Older duplicate entities are listed in a Repairs issue and left for you to delete.
- Alarm panels and zones that took their area from the panel device keep that area.
- A 0.2.0 install that was deleted and re-added is unchanged.
- New diagnostics field `legacy_registry_migration`; old unique ID bases are redacted.
- Optional `scripts/restore_entity_names.py` renames entities back to saved names (dry run
  by default, with undo).

## What changes for you
- Displayed names become area-style (for example "Main House Front Door"). Entity IDs stay.
- The panel's Network Devices list gets one new client entry; remove the old 0.1.x one when
  you like.
- 0.1.x refuses to load an upgraded entry (VERSION 2). To go back, restore your backup.

## Requirements
Requires elke27 0.3.14, which Home Assistant installs for you.
